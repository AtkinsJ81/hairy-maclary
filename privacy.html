Privacy policy

Hairy Maclary is a private assistant used only by the Atkins household. With each user's permission, it accesses their Gmail and Google Calendar to provide reminders, scheduling and family organisation.

Data is processed on a private computer in the household and is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. AI processing is performed by the assistant's model provider solely to answer household requests.

Users can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
